# Fahrschule SEZAI — independent PRDXQ concept

**This is an unsolicited, independent design concept. It is not the official Fahrschule SEZAI website and was not commissioned or approved by the business.** The business name is used only to identify the subject of this portfolio concept. No logo, customer image, review text, or other original visual asset was copied.

## Concept goals

- Put the three published learning areas (car, motorcycle, truck) into an at-a-glance class chooser.
- Separate the two branches and make lesson hours and office hours easy to compare.
- Give prospective learners a short list of questions for a first conversation, including transparent handling of prices and requirements not found in the checked public sources.
- Make phone and email actions easy to find on small screens.

The original homepage already contains extensive information, active customer reviews, two addresses, training classes, and contact details. This concept does not claim the original website is absent or generally broken. Its evidence-based improvement case is focused: the checked 1280 × 720 browser view had a horizontal scroll bar; class content is presented as a long set of detailed sections on the homepage; and its Impressum and privacy notice contain references to §5 TMG and legacy services such as Google+, Google Analytics, and AdSense that merit review against current legal and actual site setup. This is an editorial review recommendation, not a finding of legal non-compliance or proof that those services run on the live site.

## Scope and verification limits

No current prices, performance statistics, availability, appointment slot, course promise, or success rate is asserted here. The concept has no lead form, cookies, analytics, external font/image dependency, or storage of visitor information. Contact links are conventional tel: and mailto: links to public business channels. Address, dates and schedules should be confirmed with the business before any official use. Do not present this repository as an approved business asset.
